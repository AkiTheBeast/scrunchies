# Client Meeting Guide — Questions & Pricing

Use this when you sit down with your friend to discuss the real website.
The demo is your visual aid — show it on your phone/laptop as you go through these.

---

## Part 1: Questions to Ask Him

### Business Basics
1. **What do you want to call the business?** (We used "Mašnica" as a placeholder — does he have a name, or should we brainstorm one?)
2. **Do you have a logo?** If not, does he want one made? (This affects the scope and cost.)
3. **What's the full product range right now?** (We put 6 placeholder products — how many does he actually have? What categories?)
4. **What are the real prices for each product?**
5. **How long does each product actually take to make?** (We estimated 1–5 days — need real numbers.)

### Photos & Visuals
6. **Can he take good product photos?** (Ideally on a clean background, natural light. Phone is fine.)
7. **How many photos per product?** (One is minimum, 2–3 is better — different angles or colors.)
8. **Does he want lifestyle photos too?** (Someone wearing the scrunchie, a flat-lay arrangement, etc.)
9. **Does he have any existing photos from Facebook/Instagram we can use?**

### Contact & Operations
10. **Which phone number should be on the site?** (Personal or will he get a business number?)
11. **Which WhatsApp number?** (Same as phone or different?)
12. **Email address?** (Does he want a professional one like info@masnica.rs, or is Gmail fine for now?)
13. **Facebook page URL?** (Exact link)
14. **Instagram handle?** (Does he have one, or should he create it?)
15. **What are his actual working hours?** (When can customers expect replies?)

### Delivery & Payment
16. **Delivery options — confirm exactly:**
    - Does he do personal pickup? Where exactly in Pančevo?
    - Does he deliver locally? How far — Pančevo only? Belgrade too?
    - Does he ship via Pošta Srbije or a courier? Which one?
17. **Delivery prices?** (Free pickup, flat rate for local, postal rates?)
18. **Payment methods he actually accepts:**
    - Cash on pickup?
    - Bank transfer? (Which bank?)
    - Pouzeće (cash on delivery)?
    - Does he want to add online payment later?

### Custom Orders
19. **Does he take custom color/material requests?** (We said yes in the FAQ — confirm.)
20. **Is there a minimum order for custom work?**
21. **Does he do bulk/wholesale orders?** (For events, parties, corporate gifts?)

### Content
22. **His story — why did he start making hair ties?** (The "About" section needs his real story.)
23. **Does he have real customer testimonials or reviews?** (Facebook comments, messages he can screenshot with permission?)
24. **Any care instructions for the products?** (We wrote generic ones in the FAQ.)

### Technical
25. **Does he have a domain name?** (e.g., masnica.rs — this costs ~€10/year from registrars like Loopia.rs, Web.rs, etc.)
26. **Does he want a business email?** (e.g., info@masnica.rs — usually included with hosting or ~€1–2/month)
27. **How often will products change?** (Weekly? Monthly? Rarely? This affects whether he needs a CMS.)
28. **Does he want to update the site himself, or will he always come to you?**

## Part 2: Pricing Guide

You have two main options. Here's how to think about each.

### Option A: One-Time Fixed Price

You build it, hand it over, done. He pays once.

**What's reasonable for this scope:**

| Item | Price Range (EUR) | Notes |
|------|-------------------|-------|
| Website (design + code) | €150–300 | Single-page site like the demo, custom design, responsive, no CMS |
| Real product photography | €0 (DIY) or €50–100 | If you help him set up a photo shoot |
| Domain registration (1 year) | €8–15 | .rs domain from a Serbian registrar |
| Hosting setup (1 year) | €0–50 | Free options exist (Netlify, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages) or basic shared hosting ~€30–50/yr |
| **Total one-time** | **€150–350** | **Depending on scope** |

**Pros:** Simple, clear, he pays once.
**Cons:** If he needs changes later, you either do them for free (annoying) or need to charge again (awkward with friends).

**Tip:** If you go fixed price, define clearly what's included:
- X rounds of revisions (e.g., 2–3)
- Content he provides (photos, text, product info)
- A deadline for his feedback (e.g., he has 2 weeks to give you all photos/text)
- What's NOT included: ongoing changes, adding new features later

### Option B: Small Monthly Retainer

He pays a small monthly fee. You host the site, keep it updated, and make changes when he needs them.

**What's reasonable:**

| Item | Monthly Price (EUR) | What's Included |
|------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Basic plan | €15–25/month | Hosting, domain renewal included, up to 2–3 small updates per month (add/remove products, change prices, swap photos) |
| Standard plan | €25–40/month | Everything above + monthly check-in, minor design tweaks, basic SEO monitoring |

**First month or setup fee:** €100–200 (covers the initial build work — design, coding, content setup).

**Pros:**
- Predictable income for you.
- He always has someone to update the site.
- You can improve it over time (add features, better photos, etc.).
- No awkward "can you just change this one thing" conversations — it's covered.

**Cons:** He might not want a recurring cost. For a very small business, €20/month might feel like a lot initially.

### Option C: Hybrid (Recommended for Friends)

**The approach that usually works best for first-time freelancers with a friend as the client:**

1. **Build it for a reduced one-time price:** €100–150 (friend rate — you're still learning, this is your first client, and it's great portfolio material for you too).
2. **Host it for free** on Netlify/Cloudflare Pages (these are legitimately free, not a giveaway on your part).
3. **Domain:** He pays the ~€10/year directly, or you cover it as a gesture.
4. **Future updates:** Charge per task:
   - Small changes (swap a photo, update a price): free or €5–10
   - Add a new product with photos: €10–15
   - New feature or section: €30–50+
   - Seasonal redesign/refresh: €50–100

**Why this works:**
- Low barrier for him to say yes.
- You get a real project for your portfolio.
- No ongoing obligation for either of you.
- If the business grows and he needs more, you naturally grow with it.

## Part 3: What to Say to Him

Here's a rough script for the conversation:

> "Hey, I made a demo of what your website could look like — check this out [show demo on phone].
>
> This is all placeholder stuff — I'd replace it with your real products, photos, prices, and contact info.
>
> For the actual site, I'd need you to send me:
> - Good photos of each product (I can help you take them)
> - Your prices and product names
> - Which phone/WhatsApp/email to use
> - Your delivery info
>
> For pricing — I'm thinking [pick your option from above]. What do you think?
>
> The site would work on phones too [show responsive demo], and customers could order directly through WhatsApp, which you already use."

## Part 4: Before You Start the Real Build

Checklist of what you need FROM HIM before writing any code:

- [ ] Confirmed business name
- [ ] Logo (or decision to skip for now)
- [ ] Product list with: name, price, description, category, lead time
- [ ] Product photos (minimum 1 per product, ideally 2–3)
- [ ] Hero/banner photo
- [ ] About section text (his story) or enough info for you to write it
- [ ] Phone number for the site
- [ ] WhatsApp number
- [ ] Email address
- [ ] Facebook page URL
- [ ] Instagram handle (if applicable)
- [ ] Delivery options and prices confirmed
- [ ] Payment methods confirmed
- [ ] Business hours / response times
- [ ] Real customer testimonials (with permission) or skip testimonials for now
- [ ] Domain name preference (e.g., masnica.rs, or something else)
- [ ] Agreement on price and what's included

## Part 5: Free Hosting Options for a Static Site Like This

Since this site is pure HTML/CSS/JS with no backend, you can host it completely free:

| Service | Cost | Pros | Cons |
|---------|------|------|------|
| **Netlify** | Free | Drag-and-drop deploy, HTTPS, custom domain, fast CDN | 100GB bandwidth/month (more than enough) |
| **Cloudflare Pages** | Free | Unlimited bandwidth, very fast, custom domain | Slightly more technical setup |
| **GitHub Pages** | Free | Simple if you use Git already | Public repo unless you pay for GitHub Pro |
| **Vercel** | Free | Great performance | More oriented toward frameworks, but works for static too |

All of these include free HTTPS (SSL certificate), which is important for trust and SEO.

**Recommended for simplicity:** Netlify. You literally drag the folder onto their dashboard and the site is live. Then you point the domain at it.
